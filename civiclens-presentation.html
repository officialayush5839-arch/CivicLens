The Municipal Dilemma

Why Traditional Systems Fail

Citizens are left stranded in bureaucratic blind spots while operations teams drown in unlinked duplicates and silent deadline failures.

🔮

Black-Box Decisions

Opaque Triage & Misclassification

Complaints are assigned priority through simplistic keyword heuristics or inaccessible algorithms. Citizens receive zero rationale when an electrical hazard is placed in the same queue as a park bench painting.

⚠ Legacy Baseline: Missed 13 of 14 Life-Threatening Emergencies
🔄

Duplicate Chaos

Unlinked Cascade Complaints

A single water main rupture generates 50+ panicked phone calls and app submissions. Without offline NLP clustering, municipal dispatchers deploy redundant crews or lose tracking of secondary impacts.

⚠ Staff waste 40% of time reading redundant tickets
⏰

Invisible Deadlines

Silent SLA Expirations

Deadlines are either non-existent or tracked with naive wall clocks that ignore category severity. Escalations fail silently, and server restarts drop monitoring timers without any reconciliation.

⚠ Zero deterministic state mapping; unmonitored backlogs
🔒

No Audit Trail

Vulnerable to Administrative Evasion

Status changes, overrides, and closures occur in the dark. Without immutable logging, unresolved grievances are quietly deleted or marked "Resolved" without mandatory evidence or citizen appeals.

⚠ Disappearing records destroy public institutional trust

Zero-Trust by Architecture

Security by Design

Comprehensive defenses protecting citizen identities, ensuring operational integrity, and guaranteeing auditability.

🛡️
Role-Based Access
Admin • Employee • Citizen

Constructor-enforced `UserSession` prevents role spoofing. Citizen routes cannot access administrative or employee modification handlers.

🔐
BCrypt 12-Round Salt
Slow-Hashing Protection

All passwords are salted and hashed with 12 iterative rounds. Protects against offline rainbow table attacks and credential harvesting.

🚫
Zero Hardcoded Keys
External Configuration

Secrets and database connection parameters are externalized in `.env` with fallback to `config.properties`. Excluded from version control.

📝
Prepared Statements
100% Parameterized SQL

Zero string concatenation in queries across all 12 tables. Completely neutralizes SQL injection vulnerabilities in SQLite and MySQL.

👤
Ownership Guards
Cryptographic Isolation

Complainants can only read, track, or appeal complaints tied to their authenticated citizen ID. Cross-user modification attempts are strictly blocked.

🔍
Separated Notes & PII
Quarantined Staff Views

Citizen contact details are redacted from public audit records. Staff internal investigation remarks remain strictly partitioned from citizen portals.
